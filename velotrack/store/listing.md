VeloTrack: offline bike GPS

## Short description (80)

Offline cycling map and ride recorder. GPS only, no data plan, free and open.

## Full description

VeloTrack is a free, open-source bike computer for your phone. It shows an offline vector map from a tilted, course-up 3D view, records your rides and keeps your kilometres, without ever needing a data connection while you ride.

MAPS THAT LIVE ON YOUR PHONE
• Download the area you ride in straight from the app: 10 km of streets, 100 km of roads, a 1000 km region, the whole world overview or all of France. The exact size is shown before you confirm.
• Map data comes from OpenStreetMap via Protomaps and is rendered on the phone with MapLibre. Nothing is fetched while you ride.
• Dark, black-background style for OLED screens at night; a bright, high-contrast style in daylight. Switch by hand or let the app follow sunrise and sunset.

RIDE
• Records rides of any length with speed, distance, moving time, average and climb. Auto-pause when you stop, auto-start when you set off, and every ride is saved so nothing is lost.
• Riding mode: above 5 km/h every button fades away and only the essentials stay. Tap the screen to bring them back.
• Keep the screen on, dimmed to the level you choose, while a ride is recorded.
• Export any ride as a GPX file, or follow a planned GPX route with off-route alerts.

PLACES
• Home and Work buttons with straight-line guidance: a pin on the map, a dashed line and the distance and direction in the header.
• Favourites: add as many places as you like (people, restaurants, theatres, anything) with a name and a note.
• The header always tells you the name of the street, park or place you are on, read from the offline map.

STATISTICS
• Totals for all time, this year, this month and this week: distance, rides, moving time, climb and your longest ride.

PRIVACY AND BATTERY
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no Google Play Services. Your rides stay on your phone.
• Uses only the GPS receiver while riding. Works in airplane mode. Built to run all day on a large battery.
• Open source (MIT): https://github.com/fablab503-collab/fablab-tools

Requires Android 8.0 or newer and a GPS receiver. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
