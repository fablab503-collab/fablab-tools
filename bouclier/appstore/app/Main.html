Bouclier is off. Turn it on in Safari's settings.

Turn on Bouclier in Safari's settings to start blocking.

Turn on Bouclier in Settings to start blocking.

  1. Open Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  2. Tick Bouclier.
  3. Click Always Allow on Every Website… (or Edit Websites…, then Allow for other websites). Bouclier then also hides empty ad spaces, closes pop-up ads and removes video ads.
  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Turn on Bouclier, then set All Websites to Allow, so Bouclier can also hide empty ad spaces, close pop-up ads and remove video ads.
  3. In Safari, open the page menu in the address bar and tap Bouclier to see what was blocked, pause a site or hide an element.

Test Bouclier in Safari An independent test page loads real ad and tracker addresses and shows what got through.

Bouclier keeps no account and collects no data: statistics stay on this device.
