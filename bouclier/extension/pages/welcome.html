Setup check

Bouclier checks its own setup. Anything marked with an orange dot needs one click from you.

    Worth knowing

    1. The shield in the toolbar shows what was blocked on the page. Switch a site off there when it misbehaves, or pause it for 1 hour, 2 hours or until tomorrow.
    2. Bouclier in Safari's page menu (in the address bar) shows what was blocked on the page. Switch a site off there when it misbehaves, or pause it for 1 hour, 2 hours or until tomorrow.
    3. Right-click anything and choose Hide This Element… to make it disappear for good, or Pause Bouclier on this site.
    4. Hide an element in Bouclier's menu, then tap the thing you want gone. It stays hidden on that site.
    5. Shortcuts: Option-Shift-B opens Bouclier, Option-Shift-P pauses the current site, Option-Shift-H hides an element. Change them in Safari Settings → Extensions.
