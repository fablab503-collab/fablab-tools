              My filters

              One per line, in Adblock Plus syntax. Examples:

              example.com              block everything from example.com
||ads.example.net^       same, explicit form
/sponsor/*$image         block images whose address contains /sponsor/
@@||example.org^         never block example.org
lemonde.fr##.promo       hide .promo on lemonde.fr
##.sponsored-post        hide .sponsored-post everywhere
! lines starting with ! are comments

                  How it works

                  • Network blocking. Filter lists are compiled into Safari's declarativeNetRequest rules. Safari applies them itself, so Bouclier never sees the pages you visit.
                  • Element hiding. Leftover ad spaces are hidden with a stylesheet, injected only on sites you allowed Bouclier to access.
                  • Pop-ups. When a page opens a new tab to an address on the lists' pop-up filters, Bouclier closes it and tells you.
                  • Clean links. Tracking tags such as utm_source or fbclid are removed from a page's address before it loads.
                  • YouTube. A small script removes ads from YouTube's player data before the player sees them, and skips any ad that still slips through.
                  • Statistics stay on this Mac. "Data not downloaded" is an estimate that assumes about 20 KB per blocked request.
                  • Updates. The filter lists come with the app. Each Bouclier update from the App Store brings fresh lists.

                  Credits
