Why this one

Every other cycling app turns your ride into content. This one turns it into a number.

Strava wants your social graph. Google wants your location history. Komoot wants money per region. They all want the network. VeloTrack wants nothing, and the ad should demonstrate that rather than claim it.

The demonstration is airplane mode. GPS is a receiver: it listens to satellites and never speaks back. So a phone with every radio switched off still knows exactly where it is, to five metres. Most people do not know this, which is what makes it worth sixty seconds. The whole spot is one uninterrupted proof, and the flight icon stays in the status bar from the first shot to the last.

The one production rule

HOLD

No AI model can draw your app. Ask any of them for "a cycling app on a phone screen" and you get an invented interface with melted text. Every frame that shows the screen is a real recording, from the emulator or from the phone in your hand.

That splits the edit cleanly, and it means no compositing: generated shots and real screen cuts simply alternate. Where a generated shot happens to include the phone, keep it out of focus or crop above it.

Generation settings

SettingUseWhy
Kling 3.0 3–15 s · up to 4K Every generated shot here. Custom duration means the clip matches the cut instead of being trimmed.
Veo 3.1 4 / 6 / 8 s Only if you want its native audio on a single shot. Locked to 8 seconds at 1080p and above.
Image to video S02, S03 Photograph your actual bike and mount, then animate the still. The hardware in the ad is then really yours.
Character consistency S05 – S12 Train one rider from reference images so the same person appears in all six riding shots. Without it each cut is a different cyclist.
Camera presets 70+ available Named in bold at the end of each prompt. The move is doing as much work as the subject.
Aspect 2.39:1 Set it at generation. Cropping a 16:9 clip afterwards throws away the framing you asked for.

Grade and sound

  • The day passes in the grade. Cool and blue at 0:00, neutral by 0:22, warm and golden from 0:30. That arc is what makes fifteen disconnected clips read as one ride.
  • No voiceover. The spot has no claims in it, only a demonstration. A voice would turn it back into an advert.
  • Sound is bike and world. Freewheel tick, tyre on wet stone, wind, birds, a bell. Then one restrained track underneath from about 0:18.
  • Cut the music at 0:43. Silence under the proof shot. Let the ride end before the type arrives.

If you only make one thing

Shoot S01 and S13 on your own phone this week, cut them together with the screen recording of the app auto-starting, and you have a fifteen second version that says everything. The generated riding footage makes it a film; those three real shots make it true.
